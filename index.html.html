03 — How it works

A quiet addition to your routine.

i.

Fill any water bottle

Kiru screws onto any standard water bottle, home or away. Use the one that comes in the box, or the one already in your bag.

ii.

Press once. Or twice.

One press for a gentle shower. Two for a focused stream. The rotating two-layer disc nozzle gives you full control — you decide what feels right.

iii.

Carry on with your day

The device fits in your palm. Recharges in under two hours via USB-C. About a month of daily use per charge.

04 — The product

Designed to feel as good as it looks.

i.

Adjustable spray

Two-layer rotating disc nozzle. Two intensity modes — gentle shower or focused stream. Designed around female anatomy.

ii.

UVC sterilisation

A dedicated UVC button on the back of the device sterilises the water before use. Addressing hygiene at the source — and the psychological anxiety that comes with it.

iii.

Premium materials

Matte finish, soft-touch silicone grip, metallic accent ring. Belongs on your shelf, not in a drawer.

iv.

USB-C, a month per charge

LiPo battery, USB-C port at the base, IPX6 water resistant. 30–40 cleans per charge. Roughly a month of daily use.

A note from the founders

Between us, we've been daily bidet users for years.

We travel a lot. Across countries, between cities, in and out of homes that aren't ours. And every time we left home, we ran into the same problem — fixture bidets don't move with you, and nothing portable felt like something we actually wanted to use.

When one of our devices broke, we spent weeks looking for a replacement. Nothing existed. Every other part of our routines has been thoughtfully reimagined — our skincare, our supplements, our homes. But this — the most personal moment of the day — was still being served by plastic squeeze bottles or fixtures that simply don't travel.

Kiru is the product we wanted to find, and couldn't. We're building it for fellow globetrotters, frequent travellers, and anyone who already knows the difference.

Kristie & Zeng Founders
